## 1. Shopify orders created with deferred/partial payments are treated as fully paid immediate sales in analytics, messing up external ad conversions and accounting.

**Who:** Shopify merchant accepting B2B or deferred payments
**Doing about it now:** Manual reconciliation at year-end or ignoring discrepancies
**Already exists:** Shopify Analytics, native payment settings, third-party reporting apps *(from the model's knowledge - verify before building)*
**Access check:** Shopify Admin API and webhooks are freely accessible to any merchant or app developer.

**Competitor check:** partial — A specialized analytics connector or middleware that normalizes deferred and partial Shopify order values for ad platforms (GA4, Meta) and accounting tools.
